Research

I study how the spatial and spectral structure of light can be designed together, and what this makes possible: pulses that resist diffraction and travel at a chosen group velocity, incoherent light with the same properties, beams that refocus at programmed distances, and centimeter-scale optical instruments built from a single block of glass. Each topic below has its own page with the key results and publications.

Space-time wave packets

Pulsed beams in which each wavelength travels at its own angle. They propagate without diffraction, and their group velocity in free space is set by design, above or below c.

Overview and papers →

Non-separable partially coherent light

Broadband incoherent light from LEDs and superluminescent diodes, structured so that a narrow beam resists diffraction and its coherence travels at a tunable speed.

Overview and papers →

Self-imaging and 3D light shaping

Talbot and Montgomery effects: fields whose spatial frequencies are chosen so that a pattern or a tightly focused spot forms again and again at programmed distances along the beam.

Overview and papers →

Compact instrumentation with r-CBGs

Rotated chirped volume Bragg gratings spread the spectrum of light inside a block of glass, replacing the grating, lens and free space of conventional spectrometers and pulse shapers.

Overview and papers →

Optica · 2026 New

Observation of the spatially structured Montgomery effect in free space

M. Yessenov, L. Sacchi, A. Palmieri, L. A. Hall, A. F. Abouraddy, F. Capasso

The Montgomery effect generalizes Talbot self-imaging from periodic to aperiodic fields. We observe it directly: a phase-programmed beam reforms tightly focused spots at chosen distances in free space, with low background between them.

Paper arXiv
Nature Photonics · 2026 New

On-chip space–time wave packet laser array

J. Lee, H. Ren, Y. G. Liu, M. Yessenov, Y. Wei, B. Huh, …, M. Khajavikhan

An integrated semiconductor laser array emits incoherent space-time wave packets directly, without external pulse shaping. The beams are propagation-invariant and self-healing, and tuning their spatial–spectral correlations sets their width and propagation length.

Paper
Nature Communications · 2025

Ultrafast space-time optical merons in momentum-energy space

M. Yessenov, A. H. Dorrah, C. Guo, L. A. Hall, J.-S. Park, J. Free, E. G. Johnson, F. Capasso, S. Fan, A. F. Abouraddy

Merons (half-skyrmions), a topological texture, imprinted on the spectral surface of an ultrafast pulse in momentum–energy space, giving a freely propagating optical structure localized in all three dimensions.

Paper arXiv
Optics Letters · 2023

Ultra-compact synthesis of space-time wave packets

M. Yessenov, O. Mhibik, L. Mach, T. M. Hayward, R. Menon, L. Glebov, I. Divliansky, A. F. Abouraddy

A chirped volume Bragg grating rotated by 45° resolves and recombines the spectrum with no free-space propagation or collimating optics; with a phase plate between two such gratings, the synthesis setup fits in 25 × 25 × 8 mm.

Anomalous refraction of optical spacetime wave packets

B. Bhaduri, M. Yessenov, A. F. Abouraddy

Space-time wave packets crossing a planar interface between two dielectrics refract differently from conventional pulses: the change in their group velocity is set by their spatio-temporal structure, not only by the two refractive indices.

A Talbot self-imaging effect in space-time wave packets that is hidden from spatial or temporal observation alone — only visible in joint space-time intensity.
