Research

My work develops the physics and devices that give light new forms — from metalenses that reshape wavefronts at the nanoscale to space-time wave packets with propagation properties that have no classical counterpart.

Flat Optics & Metalenses

Design and fabrication of high-NA metalenses (NA > 0.8) operating across the visible spectrum. Current focus: metalenses for precision metrology and diffraction-limited imaging at the wafer scale.

Space-Time Structured Light

Spatio-temporally structured wave packets — light with tightly correlated spatial and spectral degrees of freedom — exhibit anomalous propagation, refraction, and diffraction that have no counterpart in conventional optics.

Quantum & Atom Optics

Meta-optics for reconfigurable neutral-atom arrays with >10,000 trap sites, in collaboration with the Lukin Group (Harvard) and QuEra Computing.
