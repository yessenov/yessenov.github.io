Research topic

Non-separable partially coherent light

Light from an LED or a superluminescent diode is broadband and incoherent: its spectral components have no fixed phase relationship, so it cannot be shaped into a pulse in the usual sense. Such light is normally treated as a field whose spatial and temporal properties are independent of each other, and two consequences are taken for granted: a narrow incoherent beam spreads quickly as it propagates, and its random fluctuations travel through free space at the speed of light, c.

We showed that both assumptions can be lifted by making the spatial and temporal degrees of freedom of the incoherent field non-separable. Using the same idea as for coherent space-time wave packets, we assign each wavelength of the source to a specific propagation angle. The spectral components remain mutually incoherent, but the tight correlation between spatial and temporal frequencies is enough to produce a narrow spatial feature that propagates without diffraction, and a coherence function whose peak moves at a speed set by the structure of the spectrum rather than at c.

The experiments, done during my Ph.D. at CREOL with Ayman Abouraddy, combine techniques from ultrafast pulse shaping with spatial beam modulation, applied to incoherent light from LEDs and superluminescent diodes. They show that space-time structuring of light does not require a laser or a coherent source.

Key results

  • Diffraction-free incoherent fields from an LED. Structuring the spatio-temporal spectrum of light from a light-emitting diode produces a broadband incoherent field whose intensity is a narrow spatial feature on a constant background, and the feature does not diffract. Optica 6, 598 (2019)
  • Tunable feature. Spectral engineering sets the width of the feature, switches it from bright to dark, and adjusts its amplitude relative to the background. Optica 6, 598 (2019)
  • Coherence group velocity. By analogy with the group velocity of a pulse, the speed of the peak of the coherence function defines a coherence group velocity; with a suitable spatio-temporal spectral structure it can be tuned in free space. Interferometric measurements with light from a superluminescent diode gave values from 12c to −6c. Opt. Lett. 44, 5125 (2019)
